# Gap Score (example formula supplied with the product brief)

Gap Score = 100 x [ 0.45 x N(EVs per charger) + 0.25 x (1 - Coverage Score) + 0.20 x N(Demand Context) + 0.10 x N(Corridor Deficit) ]

This is the example formula configured for the platform; it is not independently verified official government methodology.
Higher scores mean a larger mismatch between EV demand and public charging supply.
The normalisation ranges N(.), the treatment of missing values, and the definition of Demand Context are not specified in this document.
Charging stations and charging points are different units: a station can contain several charging points.
